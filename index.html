Current MVP

Receipt and expense verification platform.

Our first prototype focuses on helping organisations review expense claims more efficiently. The system aims to extract receipt data, compare it against company rules, identify inconsistencies, and support a clearer approval process.

  • Receipt upload and data extraction
  • Policy-based claim checking
  • Anomaly and inconsistency flags
  • Audit trail for review decisions
